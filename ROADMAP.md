## Hierarchical map refinement — world to local scenes (owner discussion, 2026-11-27)
Design direction: one persistent campaign world, linked map scales rather than independent generators. World overview (continents/kingdoms/major destinations); regional cartography (roads, terrain, settlements/landmarks); settlement maps (districts, streets, gates, individual buildings); local interiors/dungeons (rooms, floors, entrances and connections); tactical encounters when required (actual actor positions, distances, obstacles/terrain). World/regional retains approved ink-on-parchment artwork; settlement/interior/tactical views require suitable later-scale assets and visual acceptance, not enlarged world icons. Godot native scenes/cameras and reusable rendering nodes, no Python runtime. Use persistent location IDs and parent relationships, stable local scene topology/geometry and disclosure; revisiting loads the same established map. No arbitrary generation of unknown buildings or hidden rooms; validate descriptive proposals before making canon. Preserve B1 TURN_WORLD_CONTRACT.md global mile plane, x east/y north, nullable unknown positions. For later local maps propose explicit parent-scoped coordinate frames, feet for local/tactical geometry, floor/level and known frame links/transforms; never mix global miles with local feet or fabricate global positions. Five-foot grid optional where appropriate for tactical play, not universal. Local geometry, transitions and nested discovery need reviewed additive schema/DTO extensions; do not retrofit current B1 migration mid-assignment. Separate player-visible projection from private DM context at every scale. Clicking a known location opens information; separate Open Map action drills down when known; Travel submits intent to turn controller, never writes position directly. Fast travel may abbreviate narration on eligible known routes, but follows the same validated pace, elapsed time, resources/conditions, encounters and committed path as ordinary travel. Unknown/blocked/dangerous routes or missing mechanics need ordinary resolution/clarification, never auto-success. Browsing, zoom, drilldown and measuring do not move party. Actual transitions and red paths only from committed receipts. Do not block core DM/RAW gameplay or current A1/B1 checkpoints for local/tactical maps; A2 world/regional renderer first, settlement/interiors in later scoped tasks, tactical integration when combat/movement contracts ready. Retain original asset target and world-map scope. 03 nested persistence design, 02 travel/encounter mechanics, 04 DM local context, 01 rendering/later local assets, 05 QA, 06 packaging. Lead reconciles note into feature branches at checkpoints. Documentation only; no game, schema or publication change.
