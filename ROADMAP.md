## Owner refinement — semantic action translation and DM packet assembly (2026-11-27)
Previous Python build failed when natural phrasing did not match literal accepted verbs. Never route raw player prose into engine command dispatch, never require a keyword vocabulary or build endless synonym patches. Gameplay AI receives original unmodified input, current scene, actor/inventory/referent context and registered typed handler/argument catalogue; interprets intent semantically, maps natural phrasing to correct handler IDs and operands, resolves pronouns and context (grab/get/take/use may mean acquire, equip, consume, activate, attack or another action depending on meaning). Query only registered read-only rules/state APIs with bounded validated parameters, obtain missing facts and rule references, then propose typed actions and contextual rulings; use structured validation feedback to repair malformed proposals within bounded attempts before any mutation, never expose parser errors as ordinary gameplay, never retry a committed mechanic. Genuine consequential ambiguity asks a natural clarification; unsupported capability explicitly reported, no fake success. Multi-intent input decomposes into ordered dependent steps respecting actions/time/conditions and actual outcomes, stop at first decision/interruption, never assume remainder succeeded; B2 one-action-per-turn current limitation must be extended safely or sequence supported subturns with durable IDs before advertising multi-action support. Ordinary social/dialogue and descriptive intent need no unnecessary roll. Preserve original words and player agency.
Gameplay AI uses semantic relevance, not only rigid recency/radius/keyword matching, to assemble a bounded DM packet: original player action, interpreted intent/targets, actual committed mechanical receipts and rule references, current character and relevant resources/conditions, local scene and wider relevant regional geography/directions/routes/discoveries, present NPCs and their known relationships, quests, last few chronological turns, selected older key events and summaries, campaign/world continuity and unresolved threads relevant to current action. Query additional relevant records by stable IDs/aliases and context; use provenance, prioritize local immediate and causal relevance plus wider context, deduplicate, distinguish reported/observed facts from assumptions, avoid whole DB dumps and hidden disclosure. Separate private adjudication context from public narrator projection. Narrator receives original command plus verified answers, not bare normalized verb or canned result; free-form creative storytelling grounded in outcomes and canon. Engine owns dice, resources and authoritative writes; AI owns interpretation, contextual judgement proposals and relevance selection. No endless prompt patches; stable versioned packet and handler contracts with regression examples for varied language, ambiguous referents, multi-step intents, zero-roll conversation, older relevant memory retrieval, repair/retry and no duplicate mechanics. Implement after UI/backend wiring per preceding owner direction; current 05 QA continues unchanged, no live gameplay/version/manifest changes.
