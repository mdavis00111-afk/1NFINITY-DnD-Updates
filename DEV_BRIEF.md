## Vision and approved architecture
Previous working Python v30.5 informed fresh Godot build. Natural conversational solo D&D, inhabited believable world, ordinary adventures, player agency, no forced cosmic/chosen-one arcs or invented rules. Godot owns dice, validation, mechanical outcomes, character stats, resources, inventory/equipment, combat, movement, authoritative state and UI. SQLite structured rules and persistent state. Gemini interprets and narrates, never overrides verified mechanics. No Python runtime dependency; QA tooling permitted.
Exactly two requests for ordinary valid conversational turn: Rules-Check receives original input, relevant context and compact typed registry; Godot validates, rolls and commits; Storyteller receives unchanged original input, verified outcomes, relevant campaign/world/rules, last ten completed moves, persistent recap and existing personality/maturity instructions; returns narration, recap and structured world proposals in same response. Rejected mechanical intention one request, deterministic GUI zero, interrupted narration no repeated mechanics, no routine extraction/review/repair/summarization calls. Exceptional genuinely new item definitions or creative character generation only as previously approved. Creative freedom for NPCs, settlements, dialogue, discoveries and relationships within authoritative facts.
Map parchment black ink/quill, red travelled paths, fog and gradual discovery; stable IDs, coordinates when known, reported/observed/visited/surveyed distinct, established geography fed back, discoveries do not automatically grant ownership. Preserve approved GUI, saves, portraits, settings, campaigns. Context compact and relevant, full history stored, revision-aware caching; no claim local cache reduces provider billing. Performance measured versus expected.
