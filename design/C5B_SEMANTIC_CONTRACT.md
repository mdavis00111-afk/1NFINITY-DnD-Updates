## 1. Independent critical review
The asynchronous adapter sends two separate requests for the exact candidate and same public outcome/canon, each with a fresh durable attempt identity. The second receives no first report. Each returns candidate_hash (echo locally supplied SHA-256), contiguous coverage ranges, evidence-linked claims {kind,evidence,refs,proposal}. Local policy requires full range coverage, exact excerpts and offered public IDs, compares material assertions and effect proposals from both passes, validates scalar receipt paths against actual public outcome, rejects agency violations, private disclosure and known contradictions. Ordinary description, dialogue, NPC action, offers and hypothetical/negated claims need no deterministic grammar; they carry no mechanical patch. Coverage is a traversal check, not a proof of truth. Two calls to the selected model can still share errors; the pipeline provides practical semantic review rather than universal proof. Critical disagreement triggers bounded focused correction before preview. Verified-only recovery remains explicit. Durable reports are checked again against actual candidate/outcome before B4 commit. Early preview is distinct from committed log and never requires the player to adjudicate ordinary prose.

## 2. Contextual policy
Direct exact routine equipment selections and no-effect conversation/observe can use local validation. Other registered requests pass a durable adjudicate stage before execute, including supported spells/resources, rest, attunement, checks, travel, object interactions, transfers, story/actor/item definitions and loot. The semantic assessor gets actual proposed action, original input, public context and pinned applicable rule records, and supplies disposition, exact intent excerpt, exact committed event evidence, proposed typed ruling and reason. Local policy verifies request identity, actor/location/revision, source visibility and applicable local evidence, supported actor/state and rule availability; derives C3 clearance rather than forwarding an AI approval. C3 retains all ownership/quantity/capacity, class/slot/component, resource, rest, object and route checks. Context-dependent actions with insufficient evidence stop. Checks use RAW difficulty categories, no numeric DC supplied by the model; circumstance source events required and supported character feature state checked. Saving throws require an existing structured verified triggering DC, so arbitrary new damage/conditions/traps remain deferred. Known-duration wait can establish a tracked local epoch, not a fictional date. Long rest requires existing tracked time and C3 rest guards. No universal 6-second/10-minute RAW narrative claim. A semantic assessment can still misjudge evidence; no claim of comprehensive RAW or guaranteed semantic correctness.

## 3. Consequences
Matching required_effect claims can stage one new eligible NPC item/currency transfer or pickup/take-all from an already disclosed eligible container, with exact consent excerpt from original player text. New completed claims never display before settlement. Preserve original candidate, both reports, typed proposal and revision in C4 journal; focused Narrator correction makes the transfer pending/offer only, review that prose separately, settle original B2/B4 or standalone narrative. After deferred world reconciliation, verify original settlement and committed pending narrative, no pending adopted receipt, resolve actual unique NPC-owned definition/instance locally (no broad private inventory disclosure), persist distinct C3 subrequest ID and current revision. Contextual adjudication must cite the actual committed offer source and original consent. C3 validates ownership, quantities, local NPC, available loot and capacity and commits atomically. Recover exact subrequest before retry; no repeat debit. Only after settlement switch to a new standalone narration using verified transfer result; do not repeat original effect or recursively authorize more. Offers, declines and hypothetical claims never transfer. Newly created loot remains disclosed container until a later eligible explicit pickup; unsupported new effects corrected or blocked. Existing actor/definition discovery, geography and story proposals use C3/B4 and independent contextual assessment after narrative settlement. No generic mutation endpoint, free minting of NPC currency, forced player decision or fabricated receipt. Cancellation preserves committed effects, optional deferred skip cannot silently omit an in-flight required transfer.
