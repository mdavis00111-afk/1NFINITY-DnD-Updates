Owner approved target: five design images, four 1672×941 landscape creator pages and one 1103×1426 portrait sheet. Baseline: seven Windows v0.0.20 screenshots. Source: Project GUI Fidelity Audit & Implementation Plan, reviewed by lead developer. This is a reconstruction of approved interfaces, not a redesign or a global scale change.

## Asset handoff
The five approved PNGs are in `design/approved/`, the seven baseline PNGs are in `design/baseline-v0.0.20/`, and the full audit is in `design/GUI_FIDELITY_AUDIT.txt`. Verify them with `sha256sum --strict --check design/SHA256SUMS`. Use these files as development references. Preserve the separate runtime art and keep `design/` outside the updater’s game manifest.

## Global visual requirements
Match visual composition, panel positions, portrait sizes, ornamental gold corners and complete edges, lighting, typography hierarchy (title, heading, label, value, prose), icons, margins and gutters. Center text horizontally and vertically where appropriate; left-align readable prose, right-align numeric columns. Remove blanket font/icon/height/margin reductions only while restructuring layouts. Compact icon-led navigation and clearly illuminated active/primary actions; preserve approved hall artwork and integrate approved scenic reference art. All four creator pages fit at 1280×720 and 1920×1080 without whole-page or central-form scrolling, in windowed/maximized/fullscreen modes. Independent reference, long text, modal and list regions may scroll when needed. Preserve all controls and long text access. Do not bake interactive text or statistics into imagery.

## Page-specific requirements
1. Identity: substantial title/flourish, identity heading and guidance; labelled name, race, class, background, sex/gender, alignment with icons; ample description input and AI generation; personality, ideal, bond, flaw, backstory editable; wider ornate portrait and adjacent Choose Portrait; reference subject icon, formatted text and scenic art; functional draft and navigation.
2. Ability Scores: explicit roll 4d6 drop lowest, standard array and 27 point buy; prominent roll/reroll, six readable icon cards with base and racial adjustments, functional swap, labelled full dice history and derived stats (initiative, HP, passive perception, save DC, spell attack). Reference should reflect current choice, not stale alignment.
3. Skills & Spells: compact structured skills and saves with headers/dividers and aligned numbers; readable proficiency, spellcasting ability, DC, attack, slot counts and available/maximum selection counts; distinguish language allocation sources; functional selectors for skills, languages, subclasses, cantrips, spellbook and prepared spells; icon/name/category/description rows; current reference and preparation guidance. No missing saving throws.
4. Loadout & Review: compact identity banner, six combat statistics, structured equipment choices inside equipment panel or themed selector, inventory quantities, attacks with name/to-hit/damage/type, languages/proficiencies, features/traits, coins. Retain every required choice and save. Begin Adventure visible but disabled until campaign runtime exists; no fabricated gameplay.
5. Finished sheet: centered portrait canvas aspect approximately 1103:1426; approved sheet artwork rather than hall as sheet background; ornate portrait, title, character name, identity dividers, level badge, XP bar, combat cards, ability/saves/skills/attack columns and all equipment/inventory/spells/features/notes. No clipped modifiers or permanently truncated descriptions. Independent viewer with vertical scrolling and horizontal navigation when zoom exceeds viewport width. Fit-width default, 50–200% zoom, 10-point increments, reset 100%, gold/dark scrollbar; toolbar outside sheet, retain select/edit/archive/catalogue/exit.
