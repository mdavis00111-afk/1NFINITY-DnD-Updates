## 1. Objective and visual direction
Godot builds a dynamic persistent map from campaign-local SQL world geography, not a DM-generated image or independent procedural world generator. DM creates adventure/world details; interpretation AI extracts structured geographical facts from DM narrative; Godot validates and reconciles proposed facts, commits through SQLite and renders reusable transparent black-ink artwork. Map is part of the DM's memory. Aged parchment, quill illustrations, crosshatched terrain and shadows, handwritten location labels, red ink actual travel lines, red circles visited sites, red crosses significant discoveries/dangers, black dotted roads/tracks, fog of war, seamless pan/zoom. No modern markers, colorful tiles or normal-play coordinate grid. Match reference composition: compass, parchment edges, left vertical tools, right layer checkboxes and legend, scale bar with actual units, exploration indicator only when meaningful denominator exists; no fabricated global exploration percentage for unbounded world. Individual transparent PNG/SVG assets, deterministic variations. Do not bake example map, labels, routes or geography into background.

## 3. World state and AI interpretation
New campaign starts almost entirely unknown. Reveal only established starting location/name and actually known surroundings; no arbitrary filler geography or automatic empty settlement. DM creates facts where undefined; interpreter extracts names, types, terrain, directions/distances, duration, geographical relationships, roads/paths/rivers/crossings, discoveries/changes, reported/observed/visited status and player position from narrative. Interpreter must not independently invent features, settlements, encounters, precision or mechanical outcomes. It proposes structured typed data with source event/turn reference and request/campaign/revision identity. Godot validates all fields, reconciles stable IDs and known records, rejects cross-campaign and stale or conflicting updates, commits accepted facts with audit history. Insufficient information: record relational knowledge and unresolved/provisional coordinates; don't force precise positions, invent distances or move established towns. Semantic consistency needs validation beyond JSON shape. Resolve references using IDs, aliases, region and relationships, not global name alone. Preserve conflicting established record and flag unresolved contradiction. Failed network or validation cannot partially apply geography/movement.
## 4. SQL requirements
Extend existing per-campaign SQLite schema through reviewed migrations; preserve existing saves, IDs, opening facts and NPC records. No separate competing world authority. Location data supports campaign/world/location IDs, parent, name/aliases, kind, world x/y, precision, elevation where known, footprint, terrain, region, description, population if known, occupancy, discovery status, first discovery/last visit, NPC/quest/encounter links, asset ID, source narrative reference, notes. Separate relational routes, geographic relationships, terrain regions, discovery, movement history and audit records. Routes/rivers/travel paths support ordered multiple coordinate points. Nested locations and multiple world regions. Exact schema, indices, migrations, units, axes, fog geometry left to implementing specialists; document before code. Current x/y are nullable REAL and lack explicit unit/precision, do not assume legacy zero or origin means known. Use world-space defined distance units independent of screen/zoom, expandable without small fixed map, distance/direction/nearby/regional/route search, stable town positions; unknown stays unknown. Use parameterized SQL, transactions, durable uniqueness, visibility projections, stable IDs, bounded query result sizes. No AI SQL or executable patches.
## 5. Fog of war and discovery
Unknown (no information), Reported (mentioned), Observed (seen), Visited (physically explored), Surveyed (mapped in detail). Reported may be faint sketch, note or question mark only with sufficient positioning; unresolved knowledge in list/notes, not invented coordinates. Observed/visited/surveyed reveal according to actual knowledge; visited permanent black ink. Persistent parchment-style crosshatch/fog for unexplored, not full reveal of all secrets within visible area. Discovery independent of existence and occupancy; absence of known data does not mean world empty. Player map/legend/location search never exposes secret undiscovered information; relevant DM private context and player-visible map projections separate.
## 6. Travel and turn contract
Actual resolved movement updates actor position and persistent ordered path in same authoritative mechanical transaction as movement receipt. Roads that exist, proposed/intended travel and confirmed traversed route are separate. Red ink path only for actual travel; no movement from discussion, intentions, narration alone or interpreter suggestion. Backtrack along established geography, no silent changes. If path geometry not known, persist confirmed segment relationships and uncertainty, do not claim precise route. Narrator describes committed movement; interpretation AI extracts nonmechanical geography from narration as proposed facts. Use V024_TURN_CONTRACT.md (ActionSubmission → TurnContext → Rules-Check → ValidatedPlan → MechanicalReceipt → NarrationContext → NarrationReply → TurnView). Add a scoped geographic extraction/validation stage after narrative proposal and before accepted fact/narration persistence. Distinguish actual movement authority from descriptive discoveries; discovery proposals cannot move actor, advance time, set encounters or invent rolls. If travel needs new destination facts before resolving movement, resolve missing geography explicitly via validated proposal without executing movement; no circular authority. Idempotent retries by turn/request/receipt and revision guards; no duplicate location, route, discovery or reroll. Turn-zero opening is separate; derive starting map from committed opening only. Do not hold database transaction across provider network calls.
## 7. DM data pack
Query only relevant current location, coordinates/precision, region, surrounding terrain, nearby established locations, distances/directions, known roads/routes, visited sites, NPCs, history, active local quests, recent travel, unresolved geographic facts and relevant source references. Established geography is canon; DM may create details where undefined, not silently contradict prior geography. Bound context; don't send entire world DB or unrelated campaigns. Use SQL-backed IDs, spatial/relational retrieval; no invented precision. Example: DM says Oakhaven to old stone bridge three miles north on dirt road, narrow river with watermill on eastern bank. Extract bridge/road/river/mill relationships and evidence; record movement only if already mechanically resolved. Return weeks later using same records, not reinventing town or mill.
## 8. Godot renderer
Independent layers: (1) parchment, (2) oceans/water, (3) terrain, (4) rivers/coastlines, (5) roads/tracks, (6) settlements, (7) landmarks/adventure sites, (8) labels, (9) fog, (10) actual travel history, (11) party position, (12) annotations. Rendering of overlays must respect discovery/privacy (fog not bypassed by toggles). Stable deterministic asset selection/placement per ID/variant/geometry; no redraw randomness, no AI request to render already known map. Viewport culling, appropriate LOD and scalable geometry. Use reusable individual art; visual fidelity comparable to supplied mockup, not generic pins. Map UI accessible via existing gameplay Map tab. Mouse-wheel zoom, drag pan, centre player, overview, regional view, search, legend, layer visibility, annotations, location details, travel history, distance measurement and fullscreen. Clicking known location queries its known SQL data only. Tool actions work or explicitly unavailable until implemented, not fake buttons. No state changes from panning/zooming; annotations persist separately from canon and mechanical effects.

## Hierarchical map refinement — world to local scenes (owner discussion, 2026-11-27)
Design direction: one persistent campaign world, linked map scales rather than independent generators. World overview (continents/kingdoms/major destinations); regional cartography (roads, terrain, settlements/landmarks); settlement maps (districts, streets, gates, individual buildings); local interiors/dungeons (rooms, floors, entrances and connections); tactical encounters when required (actual actor positions, distances, obstacles/terrain). World/regional retains approved ink-on-parchment artwork; settlement/interior/tactical views require suitable later-scale assets and visual acceptance, not enlarged world icons. Godot native scenes/cameras and reusable rendering nodes, no Python runtime. Use persistent location IDs and parent relationships, stable local scene topology/geometry and disclosure; revisiting loads the same established map. No arbitrary generation of unknown buildings or hidden rooms; validate descriptive proposals before making canon. Preserve B1 TURN_WORLD_CONTRACT.md global mile plane, x east/y north, nullable unknown positions. For later local maps propose explicit parent-scoped coordinate frames, feet for local/tactical geometry, floor/level and known frame links/transforms; never mix global miles with local feet or fabricate global positions. Five-foot grid optional where appropriate for tactical play, not universal. Local geometry, transitions and nested discovery need reviewed additive schema/DTO extensions; do not retrofit current B1 migration mid-assignment. Separate player-visible projection from private DM context at every scale. Clicking a known location opens information; separate Open Map action drills down when known; Travel submits intent to turn controller, never writes position directly. Fast travel may abbreviate narration on eligible known routes, but follows the same validated pace, elapsed time, resources/conditions, encounters and committed path as ordinary travel. Unknown/blocked/dangerous routes or missing mechanics need ordinary resolution/clarification, never auto-success. Browsing, zoom, drilldown and measuring do not move party. Actual transitions and red paths only from committed receipts. Do not block core DM/RAW gameplay or current A1/B1 checkpoints for local/tactical maps; A2 world/regional renderer first, settlement/interiors in later scoped tasks, tactical integration when combat/movement contracts ready. Retain original asset target and world-map scope. 03 nested persistence design, 02 travel/encounter mechanics, 04 DM local context, 01 rendering/later local assets, 05 QA, 06 packaging. Lead reconciles note into feature branches at checkpoints. Documentation only; no game, schema or publication change.
