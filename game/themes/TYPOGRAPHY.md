# 1NFINITY D&D — approved typography (authoritative)
The actual supplied gold-letter specimen is the source, not an approximate typeface. The approved image was split into individually cropped glyphs, its grid and navy background removed, and its original gold RGB lettering retained in a transparent atlas.
The native Godot BMFont resource (arcane.fnt + arcane_atlas.png) is the primary font for the shared master theme. 129 glyph records include upper/lowercase Latin letters, numerals, symbols, selected accented characters and space; a handful of punctuation aliases were reconstructed from equivalent marks in the sheet. Glyph metrics are manually derived from the image and spacing is an approximation; absent Unicode characters use the previously bundled EB Garamond only as fallback. Bitmap resolution is finite; preserve the artwork at menu scale.
All menu and submenu text is native text rendered with the shared bitmap font. Buttons are text-only; hovering and keyboard focus brighten the text and reveal the illuminated flourish. Keep the original painted title and unobtrusive bottom-right build info. Do not substitute another primary typeface without approval.
